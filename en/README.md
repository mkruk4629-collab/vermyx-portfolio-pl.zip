## Principles

- I only work on my own systems, authorized labs or environments where I have permission.
- I do not publish flags, passwords, tokens, private keys, VPN profiles or data from other people's systems.
- I do not present educational labs as commercial experience.
- I update notes as my skills grow and track which techniques I can reproduce independently.
